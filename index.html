About

Focus Tomato uses a simple, well-known rhythm: focus for 25 minutes, rest for 5, and after four rounds take a longer 15-minute break. That's the whole idea. No feeds, no streaks, no sign-up screens. Open it, tap start, and get to work.

It was built to be small and honest: the app has no internet permission, so nothing you do in it ever leaves your phone.
